# Example 4: App store subtitle and promotional text

Invented app: a habit tracker for iPhone. The "before" copy is made up; it is not any real app's text.

## Before

> **Subtitle (30 chars):** Your Journey Starts Here
>
> **Promotional text (170 chars):** The ultimate app to transform your life! Beautiful design, powerful features and so much more. Download now and start your journey to a better you today!!!

## After

> **Subtitle (30 chars):** Habit tracker in 10 seconds a day
>
> **Promotional text (170 chars):** New: home-screen widgets. Tick off a habit without opening the app. Streaks, reminders and a weekly chart, with no account and no ads. [confirm each feature]

## What changed and why

- The subtitle names the job and uses the words people search for ("habit tracker") instead of a slogan. [SB] [LEADS]
- The promise is specific and low-effort (10 seconds a day), which raises perceived value by cutting effort. [OFF] [OW]
- Promotional text leads with what is new, a real reason to download today. [LEADS] [SB]
- Answers common objections (accounts, ads) in a few words and drops the hype ("ultimate", "transform your life"). [SUG] [WTS]
- Fits the character limits; every claim is marked [confirm] until it is checked against the current build. [WTS]
