# Example 1: SaaS homepage hero

Invented business: an invoicing tool for freelancers. The "before" copy is made up to show typical problems; it is not any real company's text.

## Before

> **Welcome to the Future of Invoicing**
> We are a passionate team building innovative, all-in-one financial solutions that empower businesses of every size to streamline their workflows.
> [Learn more]

## After

> **Get paid 9 days faster, without chasing a single invoice** [confirm the 9-day figure from product data]
> Send an invoice in 60 seconds. Your client pays by card in one tap, and polite reminders go out on their own until the money lands.
> Used by 4,200 freelancers [confirm] · Free for your first 5 invoices
> [Send your first invoice free]

## What changed and why

- Opened with the reader's outcome (getting paid sooner), not the company or "Welcome to". [WTS] [SB]
- Swapped category words ("innovative, all-in-one solutions") for specifics: 60 seconds, one tap, 9 days. Every number is flagged [confirm] until it is checked. [OW] [SUG]
- Pushed the reminder feature through "so what?" until it became a benefit: no chasing. [WTS]
- Answered the first objection ("is it worth trying?") with proof and a free starting point right under the headline. [SUG] [OFF]
- One specific call to action that says what happens, instead of a vague "Learn more". [LEADS] [WTS]
