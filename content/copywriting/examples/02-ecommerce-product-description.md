# Example 2: E-commerce product description

Invented product: an insulated steel travel mug sold on a small online shop. The "before" copy is made up; it is not any real brand's text.

## Before

> Introducing our premium, high-quality travel mug! Crafted from the finest materials, this amazing mug features double-wall vacuum insulation, a leak-proof lid and a sleek modern design. Perfect for everyone. Buy now!

## After

> **Coffee that's still hot at your 11am meeting.**
>
> - Hot for 6 hours, cold for 12 – double-wall vacuum steel [confirm test figures]
> - Throw it in your bag: the lid locks shut, so no coffee on your laptop
> - Fits car cup holders and most coffee-machine spouts (7.5 cm base, 18 cm tall) [confirm]
> - Dishwasher-safe lid; the body rinses clean in seconds
>
> Pour it at 7, sip it at 11. If it ever leaks, send us a photo and we'll replace it. [confirm the policy]
>
> 350 ml · 290 g · stainless steel · free returns within 30 days [confirm]

## What changed and why

- Led with a scene from the buyer's day (the 11am meeting) instead of "premium, high-quality". [SUG] [WTS]
- Each bullet is benefit first, spec as proof ("lid locks shut" → no coffee on your laptop). [WTS] [SB]
- Answered the real objections (leaks, fit, cleaning) where the buyer would ask them. [SUG]
- Replaced the hype words (amazing, finest, perfect for everyone) with specific numbers. [OW] [SB]
- Added a named, concrete risk reducer instead of "Buy now!"; a guarantee only if the shop can honor it. [OFF]
