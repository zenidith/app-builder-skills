# Example 5: Newsletter subject lines and a social post

Invented business: a small online pottery course with a weekly newsletter. The "before" copy is made up.

## Before

> **Subject lines:** "October Newsletter" / "News and updates from the studio" / "Don't miss out!!!"
>
> **Social post:** We are so excited to announce that our amazing beginner pottery course is now open for enrolment! Click the link in bio to learn more. #pottery #ceramics #handmade

## After

> **Subject lines:**
> - "Why your first bowls crack (and the 2-minute fix)"
> - "3 glazes that forgive beginner mistakes"
> - "12 seats left for the November cohort" [only if true; confirm the number]
>
> **Social post:**
> Most first bowls crack in the kiln. Not because of the clay.
>
> It's trapped air. Wedge the clay for two minutes and it goes away.
>
> We teach that in lesson one, along with the 4 other mistakes almost every beginner makes. The next beginner course starts Nov 3 [confirm], online, 6 weeks, 20 minutes a day.
>
> Link in bio if you want a spot.

## What changed and why

- Subject lines work as mini headlines: specific, a benefit or curiosity, under 50 characters. [SB] [OW]
- Scarcity is used only if real, with the actual number, never an invented countdown. [OFF] [WTS]
- The post opens with a surprising, concrete fact that works alone in the preview (hook), then delivers the promised fix (reward) before the ask. [LEADS] [OW]
- Gives value first and puts the single call to action at the end, after the most useful moment. [LEADS]
- Replaced "so excited" and "amazing" with practical facts: start date, format, length, daily time. [WTS] [SUG]
