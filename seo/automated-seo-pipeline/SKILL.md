# your scheduling tool Autonomous SEO & Publishing Pipeline (`your scheduling tool-seo-pipeline`)

An autonomous, end-to-end SEO strategist, long-form article architect, and multi-channel distribution engine for Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, and terminal agents. Replaces external commodity generation APIs with direct, native integration into the **a social media scheduling API** under the **your content site** brand entity.

Given a single website URL, this skill discovers high-value commercial and informational topics, reverse-engineers what is winning on Google and AI search engines (AI Overviews, Perplexity, SearchGPT), builds superior outlines with verified Information Gain, and publishes/amplifies content across connected CMS and social channels.

---

## Operating Philosophy & Modern Search Guardrails

1. **Information Gain Over Commodity Echoes:** Never repeat what the top 5 results already say. Every article must provide net-new data (e.g., regional broth chemistry, ticket machine guides, authentic pricing, translated ordering etiquette).
2. **Answer-Engine Optimization (AEO / GEO):** Provide a direct, high-density 40–60 word answer block immediately below every primary question H2 to win citations in Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and ChatGPT.
3. **Patent US9031929B1 Compliance:** Anchor every piece to the distinct `your content site` brand entity. Cultivate direct navigational intent to keep the quality ratio high.
4. **Strict Two-Gate Approval:**
   - **Gate 1:** Content Plan Approval (Clusters, keywords, priorities).
   - **Gate 2:** Outlines & Information Gain Angles Approval.

## Phase 1 — Domain & Entity Profiling

**Goal:** Establish brand voice, target audience, local context, and index footprint to prevent cannibalization.

1. **Crawl & Extract:**
   - Fetch the root URL, `/about`, `/menu` or `/guide`, and `/sitemap.xml`.
2. **Compile Brand Dossier:**
   - **Entity Identity:** Primary brand (`your content site`), niche authority (Tokyo and regional Japanese ramen culture, shop etiquette, travel logistics).
   - **Target ICP:** Inbound international travelers, culinary tourists, expatriates, and ramen connoisseurs seeking authentic shop recommendations and ordering guidance.
   - **Brand Voice:** Grounded, culturally accurate, practical, zero tourist hype, direct, respectful of shop masters.
   - **Existing Index Footprint:** Map all currently indexed pages and sitemap URLs to build a clean baseline.

---

## Phase 2 — Competitor Discovery & Topical Clustering

**Goal:** Discover 10–30 high-value keywords with verified search and LLM demand that the site has not yet covered.

### Step 2A — Competitor Discovery
1. Search Google for core niche topics:
   - `"tokyo ramen guide"`
   - `"best tonkotsu ramen fukuoka"`
   - `"ramen ticket vending machine guide"`
   - `"japan food tourism ramen"`
2. Identify 3–5 active competing publications and blogs.
3. Fetch competitor sitemaps or blog indexes and construct the **Competitor Content Map**.

### Step 2B — Gap Analysis
- **Universal Gaps (High Priority):** Fundamental topics covered by every competitor that `your content site` lacks.
- **Emerging Angles (Medium Priority):** High-intent topics covered by only 1–2 competitors (e.g., contactless payment rollout at traditional ticket machines).
- **Unaddressed Voids (Strategic Opportunity):** Niche user problems completely ignored by competitors (e.g., navigating custom noodle firmness *katame* vs. *barikata* across regional dialects).

### Step 2C — Content Plan Architecture
Organize discovered topics into structured clusters:

```
CONTENT PLAN: example-site.com

Cluster: [REGIONAL STYLES & DISCOVERY]
- Pillar: "Tokyo Ramen Styles Explained: From Shoyu to Tsukemen" ──► Ultimate Guide ──► Priority: HIGH
- Support: "Best Morning Ramen (Asa-Ramen) Spots in Tokyo" ────────► Guide ───────────► Priority: HIGH
- Support: "Tonkotsu vs Tori Paitan: Broth Differences & Origins" ──► Comparison ──────► Priority: MED

Cluster: [TRAVELER UTILITY & ETIQUETTE]
- Pillar: "How to Order Ramen in Japan: Ticket Machines to Slurping" ──► Ultimate Guide ─► Priority: HIGH
- Support: "Cashless vs Cash-Only: Tokyo Ramen Payment Rules" ───────► Practical Guide ─► Priority: MED

Article Size Mapping:
• Pillar / Ultimate Guide: ~2,500 words (size: xl)
• Practical / How-To Guide: ~1,500 words (size: lg)
• Comparison / Versus: ~1,200 words (size: lg)
• Focused Explainer / FAQ: ~800 words (size: md)
```

🛑 **GATE 1:** Present the Content Plan and **WAIT for user approval** before proceeding to SERP analysis.

## Phase 3 — SERP Reverse-Engineering & Information Gain Outlining

**Goal:** Deconstruct the top 5 ranking results for approved topics and engineer outlines that outrank them through proprietary data and direct answers.

### Step 3A — SERP & AI Overview Reverse-Engineering
For each approved keyword:
1. Fetch the top 5 Google search results.
2. Note the common baseline headings and mandatory questions.
3. Query the topic in an LLM search engine (SearchGPT / Perplexity / AI Overview) and record:
   - Which specific URLs are cited as source authorities?
   - What direct answer summary is generated?
4. Identify gaps: Missing Japanese menu translations, outdated price estimates (e.g., pre-inflation ¥800 vs. current ¥1,000–¥1,300), lack of transit directions.

### Step 3B — Information Gain Formulation
Add at least two proprietary elements absent from the competitor SERP:
- Specific Japanese phrases written in Kanji, Hiragana, and Romaji.
- Exact etiquette rules verified directly with local practices.
- Accurate 2026 pricing and payment capabilities (IC card / QR / Cash).

### Step 3C — Construct Outline (H1–H3)
- Max 10–12 H2 sections.
- 1–4 H3 subsections under key analytical sections.
- Mandatory FAQ section structured for `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema.

🛑 **GATE 2:** Present the outlines with Information Gain angles and **WAIT for user approval** before generating content and triggering your scheduling tool.

### Step 4A — Draft & Content Assembly

- Direct 40–60 word answer box beneath the primary H2.
- Clean semantic HTML / Markdown formatting with responsive comparison tables.
- Contextual internal links to other `your content site` pillar pages.
- Embedded `Article` and `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema.
