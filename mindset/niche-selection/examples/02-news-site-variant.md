# Example 2: a daily news site idea

Invented city, invented numbers, to show the news-site variant. Not research on a real market.

## The idea
An owner admires a long-running local tabloid that covers crime and scandal in a big city and wants
"something like that, semi-automated". Two candidates are scored against each other:
- **A. Crime and scandal translations** from the city's local-language press.
- **B. "What changed" for visitors and new residents**: rule changes, fees, transport, openings and
  closings, events, weather and safety alerts, with what each means for the reader.

## Daily sources
- A: other publishers' crime stories (secondary, copyrighted), police notices (primary, sparse).
- B: transport operators' notices, city and national government releases, venue and event
  listings, tourism board news, company press releases, open data (all primary, daily).

## Scores

| | A. Crime translations | B. What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Search volume | 3 — spiky, news-driven | 3 — steady practical questions |
| 2. Competition | 3 — established sites own it | 4 — 40+ easy "new rule / new fee" topics |
| 3. Monetization | 2 — ad-restricted subjects | 4 — travel and relocation buyers |
| 4. Ease of content | 3 — easy to find, risky to write | 4 — clear primary sources |
| **Total** | **11** | **15** |
| Red flags | copyright of translations, defamation, ad limits, scaled rewriting | YMYL care on safety alerts |
| **Decision** | no-go | **go** |

## B's pipeline
Sources → collect every morning → remove duplicates → draft a short summary in the site's own words
with the source linked and "what it means for you" → owner approves, edits or skips each item
(about 20 minutes a day; safety and money items always reviewed) → publish with byline and date →
newsletter and social → monthly review of visits and revenue per post.

## Linking
Practical stories link to the owner's sister sites only where a reader needs the next step (a
guide, a tool, a booking page); the about page names the family of sites; no footer link blocks.
